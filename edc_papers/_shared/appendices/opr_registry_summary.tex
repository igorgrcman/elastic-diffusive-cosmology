\label{sec:appendix-opr-summary}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Open Problem Registry Summary}

This appendix summarizes the status of open derivation problems tracked during
Book 2 development. The OPR system ensures epistemic transparency by explicitly
marking what is derived [Der], conditionally derived [Dc], or postulated [P].

\subsection*{Scale Taxonomy}

Four distinct length/energy scales appear in the EDC weak-sector framework:

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}llll@{}}
\toprule

\midrule

$\ell$ & Domain support & Sturm--Liouville interval for OPR-21: $\mu = M_0\ell$ & [P] \\
$R_\xi$ & Diffusion scale & Coordinate/correlation length: $R_\xi = \hbar c/M_Z$ & [BL] \\

\end{center}

\textbf{Unit conversion}: 1 fm $= 5.0677$ GeV$^{-1}$.

% --- Assumption Labels ---
\subsection*{Assumption Labels (A1--A3)}

Three scale-identification assumptions are tracked explicitly:

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}lll@{}}
\toprule
ID & Assumption & Use Case \\
\midrule
(A1) & $\Delta = \delta$ & Kink width = boundary-layer scale \\
(A2) & $\delta = R_\xi$ & Boundary-layer = diffusion scale \\
(A3) & $\ell = n\Delta$ with $n = O(1)$ & Domain size proportional to kink width \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\textbf{Rule}: No derivation may silently assume any of (A1)--(A3). Each must be
explicitly tagged [P] with assumption label.

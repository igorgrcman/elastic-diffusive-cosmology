10.6.3     Why Large Angles? CKM vs PMNS

Qualitative explanation [I]:
     • Neutrinos are delocalized edge modes (large κ−1 , spread over interface)
     • Quarks are localized interior modes (small κ−1 , tightly bound)
     • Larger delocalization → larger wavefunction overlaps → larger mixing angles
This explains why PMNS angles (∼ 30–45◦ ) are much larger than CKM angles (∼ 13◦ , 2◦ , 0.2◦ ).

10.7      Dirac vs Majorana

Whether neutrinos are Dirac or Majorana remains experimentally undetermined [BL].
     In the edge-mode ontology:
     • Dirac: Distinct ν and ν̄ edge modes with opposite quantum numbers
     • Majorana: Single self-conjugate edge mode
     Status: [Open] — EDC accommodates both but makes no prediction.

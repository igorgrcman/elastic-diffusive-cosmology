14.2.1     Assumption Labels for Scale Identifications
Throughout EDC derivations, the following identifications may be assumed. Each must be
explicitly labeled when invoked.

     Scale Identification Assumptions

     (A1) ∆ = δ — Kink width equals boundary-layer scale

     (A2) δ = Rξ — Boundary-layer scale equals diffusion scale

     (A3) ℓ = n∆ with n = O(1) — Domain size proportional to kink width

     Rule: No derivation may silently assume any of (A1)–(A3). When any is used, it must
     be tagged [P] with explicit label (e.g., “assuming (A2)”).



14.3       OPR-01: Membrane Tension and Mass Scale
     Epistemic Status: OPR-01

     This section derives the relationship between the bulk mass scale M0 and the membrane
     tension σ:
        • M0 = f (σ, ∆, y) derived from domain-wall physics [Dc]
        • Domain-wall ansatz for M (ξ) remains [P]
        • Yukawa coupling y remains [P]
     What is NOT claimed: We do not derive σ, ∆, or y from first principles. This derivation
     shows how these parameters are related, converting three independent [P] parameters into
     two independent + one derived [Dc].

Chapter 15

BVP Framework

     Many EDC predictions reduce to boundary value problems (BVPs). This chapter
     presents the mathematical framework and numerical methods.


15.1     Introduction: Why Boundary Value Problems?
Physical role of the BVP. The BVP determines the allowed transverse mode spectrum and
normalization, which enter overlap integrals and effective 4D couplings.
   In EDC, particle properties emerge from the geometry of 5D structures. Mathematically, this
means solving equations of the form:

                       L[ψ] = 0 with boundary conditions at y = ±δ/2                     (15.1)

    where L is a differential operator and y is the coordinate transverse to the brane.
    Key applications:
    • Fermion wavefunctions → mass eigenvalues
    • Boson localization → MW , MZ
    • Tunneling rates → decay lifetimes
    • Mode spectrum → generation structure
    Coordinate note: In this chapter we use y as the transverse BVP coordinate. It is identical
to the canonical transverse coordinate ξ used elsewhere in Part II (see Appendix B: y ≡ ξ).

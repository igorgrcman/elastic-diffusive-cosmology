10.8      Falsification Criteria
   What Would Falsify the Edge-Mode Picture

       1. Large neutrino mass (mν > 1 eV): Would require ∆ξ/κ−1 < 14, inconsistent with
          electron mass.

       2. Right-handed weak coupling: Boundary conditions would be wrong; V−A chap-
          ter fails.

       3. 4th light neutrino: Z3 structure would fail.

       4. No edge-mode solution in BVP: The thick-brane eigenvalue problem might have
          no boundary-localized eigenstate.

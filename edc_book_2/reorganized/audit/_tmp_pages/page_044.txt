5.3.2     Why Exactly Three
     Three Generations: Not Four, Not Two

     Geometric necessity [P]:
        • Z3 has exactly 3 elements
        • Each element labels one generation
        • A 4th generation would require Z4 or higher symmetry
        • This would change the entire geometric structure
     Falsifiability [Open]: If a 4th generation is discovered, the Z3 model is falsified. Current
     LHC limits exclude 4th generation with SM couplings.

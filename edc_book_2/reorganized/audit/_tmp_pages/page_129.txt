Beyond the Weak Sector

   Epistemic Warning

   This chapter is exploratory. Unlike the preceding chapters, which derived results with
   explicit epistemic tags, this epilogue discusses future directions that are:
      • Speculative — not yet subjected to rigorous derivation
      • Incomplete — missing error budgets and falsification criteria
      • Motivational — intended to suggest research directions
   Claims in this chapter should be read as [I] (identifications) or [P] (postulates) unless
   explicitly noted otherwise. They do NOT carry the epistemic rigor of Chapters 1–16.



18.1     What We Have Established
This book has shown that the EDC framework, developed in Part I for strong interactions and
mass ratios, extends naturally to the weak sector:
   • Neutron decay: Junction relaxation through geometric barrier [Dc]
   • V−A structure: Chiral localization from boundary conditions [Dc]
   • Weinberg angle: sin2 θW = 1/4 from Z6 symmetry [Dc]
   • Three generations: Angular sectors from Z3 ⊂ Z6 [Dc]
   • CKM hierarchy: Wavefunction overlap model [Dc]
   • Neutrino suppression: Edge mode localization [Dc]
    All these emerge from the same 5D membrane geometry. Note: these are [Dc] (conditional
derivations), not pure [Der], because they require the reduction dictionary for identification with
observations.

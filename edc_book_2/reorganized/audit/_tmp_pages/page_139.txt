B.8     Symbol Disambiguation
The symbol κ appears with two distinct meanings:


        Symbol       Meaning                                       Dimension      Chapters
            κ        Localization width (wavefunction decay)          length        10, 12
           κT        Fermion–stress coupling                         1/mass3          11


   Context disambiguates: κ in exponential profiles exp(−|ξ|/κ) is the localization width; κT in
m(ξ) ∼ κT T ξξ is the stress coupling.

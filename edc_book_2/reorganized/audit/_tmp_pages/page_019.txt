1.2.1   Proton: Y-Junction at 120◦
   • Topology: Three strings meeting at 120◦ angles (Steiner minimum)

   • Symmetry: S 3 × S 3 × S 3 (three-fold)

   • Energy: Proportional to total string length 3ℓp

   • Stability: Topologically protected by junction boundary conditions

   • Part I Derivation: Chapter 4 (Confinement and the Strong Force)

1.2.2   Neutron: Asymmetric Y-Junction at 60◦
   • Topology: Asymmetric junction (60◦ angle, not 120◦ ) [Der]

   • Key Feature: Metastable (can relax to 120◦ = proton) [Der]

   • Relaxation: Releases energy ∆Enp = 8Ee /π [Der]

   • Identification [Dc]: This relaxation is identified with beta decay (dictionary: junction
     → baryon, relaxation → weak decay)

   • Part I Derivation: Chapter 4 (Confinement and the Strong Force)

1.2.3   Electron: Spherical Defect (B3 Vortex)
   • Topology: Spherical membrane defect (B3 topology)

   • Energy: Surface energy ∼ σre

   • Classical Radius: re = 2.818 fm [BL]

   • Stability: Topologically stable (no decay channel)

      This bridge chapter connects the established results from Part I (“Quantum Mechanics
      and Gravity from a Unified 5D Membrane Action”) to the weak interactions developed
      in Part II. Part I established the 5D geometric framework, derived confinement from
      string tension, and obtained fundamental constants (mp /me , α) from pure geometry.
      Part II extends this framework to the weak sector. If you have read Part I, this
      chapter provides a quick summary. If you have not, it gives you the essential results
      to understand Part II.

Dictionary boundary (applies throughout): In this volume, Part I results are treated as
model inputs. Whenever a Part I quantity is identified with a baseline observable (PDG/CO-
DATA), that identification is tagged [Dc] per the boundary rule (see “On Certainty” section).
Internal model ratios remain [Der].

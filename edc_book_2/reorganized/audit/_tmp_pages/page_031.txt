2.3     The Thick Brane Necessity
Why do weak interactions require a thick brane? This section explains the physical necessity of
finite brane thickness for weak processes.

2.3.1   Thin Brane vs Thick Brane
   • Thin brane (δ → 0): Mathematical idealization. Fields strictly confined to a 4D hy-
     persurface. Works for electromagnetic and strong interactions where particles are fully
     brane-localized.
   • Thick brane (δ > 0): Physical model with finite thickness in the extra dimension. The
     brane has two distinct interfaces:
       1. Bulk-facing side: Where 5D bulk fields couple to brane dynamics
       2. Observer-facing side: Where effective 3D/4D physics emerges

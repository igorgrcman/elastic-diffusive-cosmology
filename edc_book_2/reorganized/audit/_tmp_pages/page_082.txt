11.5      Toy Model: Overlap Suppression
The previous sections gave exact solutions. Now let us build physical intuition with a simple
toy model. The goal is to see how much the right-handed coupling is suppressed, without using
Standard Model numbers.

     Toy Model Disclaimer

     This subsection contains new equations that illustrate the overlap mechanism. These are
     [P] toy-model assumptions, not derived from first principles. The purpose is pedagogical:
     to show how exponential suppression emerges from simple profiles.

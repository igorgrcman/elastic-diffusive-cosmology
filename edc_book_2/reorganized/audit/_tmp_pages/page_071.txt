10.5     V−A from Boundary Conditions
Chapter 11 derived that boundary conditions select left-handed fermions [Dc]. This applies
directly to neutrinos:

   Left-Handed Neutrinos
   The boundary conditions that select left-handed charged leptons simultaneously select
   left-handed neutrinos:

                            PL ψν = ψν,L    (normalizable edge mode)                      (10.9)

   Normalizability condition (scope guardrail):
                   Z ∞
                       dξ |fL (ξ)|2 < ∞ (flat metric, half-line domain)                  (10.10)
                       0

   This holds in the flat 5D / half-line limit. Warped metrics require additional analysis.
   Right-handed neutrinos, if they exist, are expelled into the bulk and do not couple to weak
   vertices.
   Status: [Dc:Approx] — Valid within flat + half-line scope; no additional assumptions
   beyond Chapter 11.

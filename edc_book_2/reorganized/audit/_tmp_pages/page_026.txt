Falsification and Tension Criteria
Definitions:

     • Tension: measured value lies outside estimated correction envelope (error budget not
       closed).

     • Falsification: after closure, residual deviation exceeds 3σ and no plausible Cred restores
       agreement.

    Scope guardrail: The statement “no results in tension” refers to results with completed
error-budget tables in this edition; items without closed budgets remain [Open] and are not
counted as “validated”.

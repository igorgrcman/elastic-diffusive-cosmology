where Λ is a characteristic mass scale (e.g., m0 or 1/λ from the profile). The localization

                                                RLR ∼ e−Cµ                                             (11.25)

where C > 0 is a model-dependent O(1) coefficient determined by the detailed shape of the
profile/potential and admissible boundary conditions. The coefficient C is [OPEN] until V (ξ)
and BCs are derived from the 5D action. Update: The potential structure VL , VR and Robin
BCs have been derived in Chapter 15; C remains model-dependent via the profile shape.


The suppression inequality (parameter-free form). Combining Eqs. (11.23) and (11.25)
yields the parameter-free inequality target:

                                1
                          µ>      ln(103 )      with C = O(1) ⇒ µ = O(5–10)                            (11.26)
                                C

This is the closure target: the barrier parameter must lie in a robust O(5–10) regime for generic
C = O(1).2 We do not assume C; the only claim is the inequality target. Determining C is
delegated to the BVP closure pack (OPR-21, Ch. 14).


Interpretation.
   • µ measures the “cumulative mass barrier” the RH mode must cross to reach the brane.
   • µ = O(5–10) is a modest requirement—roughly 5–10 e-foldings in the wavefunction before
     it becomes negligible (depending on C).
   • For a linear mass profile m(ξ) = m0 z/λ, this requires m0 z∗ /λ = O(5–10), i.e., the charac-
     teristic depth must be several times the localization scale.


What Must Be Derived (OPEN)

     Closure Condition: Derive µ from Membrane Parameters

     Status: [OPEN]
     The barrier parameter µ depends on:
       1. The mass profile m(ξ), which must be derived from Plenum-fermion coupling
          (Sec. 11.4)
       2. The integration cutoff z∗ , which depends on domain geometry (half-line vs finite
          interval)
       3. The scale Λ, which must connect to membrane parameters (σ, re , Rξ )
     Closure requires:
        • Express µ and C in terms of (σ, re ) from Part I
        • Show µ > ln(103 )/C follows from membrane physics, not from tuning
        • Verify robustness under domain and BC variations
     Without this derivation: V–A is qualitatively established but quantitative suppression
     remains a postulated regime [P].

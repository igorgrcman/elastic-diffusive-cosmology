11.10       Summary
     Three Takeaways (5D Cause → 3D Shadow)

       1. 5D cause: Chirality is geometry. Plenum inflow picks a sign for the mass
          profile; that sign determines which chirality localizes at the boundary. This is the
          5D mechanism.

       2. 3D shadow: V–A emerges from overlap. Gauge fields at the brane couple only
          to modes overlapping the boundary. Left-handed modes peak there; right-handed
          are displaced. The result is the observed V–A structure—the 3D projection of 5D
          localization.

       3. No new parameters. This mechanism uses standard 5D Dirac physics [BL] plus
          one EDC postulate (inflow direction) [P]. No chirality-specific coupling constants
          are introduced.


     What Remains Open

        • Gauge symmetry origin: Why SU(2)L specifically? (OPR-17, partial)
        • W± /Z0 masses: Higgs mechanism not derived from EDC.
        • Fermi constant: Quantitative GF requires thick-brane profile (Ch. 11).
        • Mixing matrices: CKM/PMNS from generational overlaps (OPR-18).
        • Neutrino mass: Majorana vs. Dirac structure (OPR-07).

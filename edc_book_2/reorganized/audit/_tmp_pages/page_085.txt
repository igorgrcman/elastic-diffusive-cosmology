   Reader Takeaway: Not a Fit, but a Closure Target

   This subsection does not fit parameters to achieve RLR < 10−3 . Instead, it states:
      1. The empirical baseline: RLR < 10−3 from experiment [BL]
      2. The mathematical consequence: µ ≳ 3.5 (barrier parameter)
      3. The closure target: derive µ from membrane physics [OPEN]
   This is the required regime for the mechanism to match the empirical baseline.
   If the derived membrane parameters yield µ < 3.5, the model would be in tension with
   experiment. If µ ≫ 3.5, the suppression is stronger than required (consistent, but not a
   prediction).
   The goal is to show that membrane physics naturally produces µ ≳ 3.5, not to choose µ
   to make V–A work.
